The ABN Rule
Advance Beneficiary Notice of Noncoverage
Before
the test
WHEN MEDICARE MAY DENY A BLOOD TEST
Ordered too frequently Not medically necessary
CMS FORM CMS-R-131 · ABN
A valid ABN must list:
The specific test
The reason it may be denied
An estimated cost + your claim choice
Given BEFORE your blood is drawn
Not a vague blanket waiver
